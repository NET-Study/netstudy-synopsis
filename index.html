The four Gospels, one at a time. Not a harmony that blends them into one voice, but a synopsis that lets each Evangelist speak in his own order.

How to read it

  • Each row is one pericope. Columns are whole passages, not spliced verses.
  • Hover a verse to light up its echoes in the other Gospels. Click to pin the comparison.
  • Toggle a Gospel off to widen the others. Fill all four keeps empty columns visible so you can see who is silent.
  • Each screen is one pericope, one Gospel at a time. Swipe sideways to move between Gospels, or tap the tabs above the text.
  • Tap a verse to pin it and open a sheet of its closest wording in the other Gospels. Tap a parallel to jump to it.
  • The ☰ button opens the outline; the arrows beside the title step through pericopes.
